## Prompts (image ChatGPT → vidéo Google Flow, image en départ)
Fin commune de chaque prompt vidéo : She speaks French with a soft Burkinabè accent, clear and natural pace. Ambient sound only, no music, no subtitles, no text on screen. 8 seconds.
Prononciation : écrire « I.A. » et « Wilfrid » dans le prompt (le tunnel affiche la bonne orthographe).

1 accroche — IMG : [FICHE] Busy street in Ouagadougou at golden hour, motorbikes blurred behind her. Waist-up shot, she holds a smartphone at chest height. — VID : Slow dolly-in from waist-up to close-up. She raises her phone with a playful smile, then looks straight into the camera and says: "Salut, moi c'est Sadia… et je suis une I.A. ! En deux minutes, je te montre comment faire pareil. On y va ?"
2 sens — IMG : [FICHE] Colorful open-air market, fabrics and products on stalls. Wide shot, she stands in the middle holding a small product box. — VID : Drone shot descending and moving forward from a wide view of the market to a waist-up shot of her. She shows the box to the camera, then says: "Des vidéos comme moi, les marques les paient. Toi aussi, tu aimerais gagner de l'argent avec ça ?"
3 simple — IMG : [FICHE] Family courtyard with a mango tree, she sits on a wooden bench. Close-up, phone in both hands, screen toward camera. — VID : Starts on a close-up of the phone screen in her hands, then tilts up to her face. She smiles and says: "Pas d'ordinateur, pas de caméra : ton téléphone suffit. Même une grand-mère y arrive. Tu veux la voir ?"
4 grand-mère — IMG : Burkinabè grandmother about 70, grey hair under a patterned wax headwrap, colorful wax dress with a closed neckline, reading glasses, sitting on a low wooden stool in a sandy courtyard, proudly holding a smartphone. Close-up, photorealistic, vertical 9:16. — VID : Static camera with a slight handheld feel. She laughs, raises the phone, points at the camera and says playfully, in French with an elderly Burkinabè accent: "Hé ! Si moi, à mon âge, j'y arrive… toi aussi tu peux ! Tu veux ta première leçon gratuite ?"
5 avant-leçon — IMG : [FICHE] Cozy living room in the evening, warm lamp light, she sits on a sofa. Chest-up shot, phone held out toward the camera. — VID : Slow zoom in. She turns the phone screen toward the camera, then says warmly, nodding at the end: "Voilà ta première leçon ! Elle est courte. Tu la regardes jusqu'au bout, d'accord ?"
6 écart — IMG : [FICHE] Rooftop terrace at night overlooking the city lights of Ouagadougou. Wide shot, she stands by the railing holding her phone. — VID : Drone orbit around her, starting wide and ending waist-up facing her. Serious then smiling, she says: "Aujourd'hui, tu regardes les vidéos des autres. Dans quelques jours, c'est toi qui les crées. Tu veux voir comment ?"
7 formation — IMG : [FICHE] Modern café terrace in the afternoon, a smartphone on a small tripod and a ring light on the table. Waist-up shot, she sits at the table. — VID : Lateral tracking shot from left to right along the table. She counts on her fingers while speaking, then looks into the camera: "Huit vidéos courtes sur ton téléphone, un exercice après chacune, et un groupe pour t'aider. Tu prends ta place ?"
8 whatsapp — IMG : [FICHE] In front of a bright wall painted green and yellow. Close-up, big smile, she holds her phone showing a green chat screen (no logo). — VID : Starts on a close-up of her smile, slow zoom out to waist-up. She taps her phone, then points down toward the bottom of the frame and says: "Super ! Appuie sur WhatsApp, tu paies, et Wilfrid t'ajoute à la formation. Tu le fais maintenant ?"
